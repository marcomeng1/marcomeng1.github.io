Videos on the Projects page are shown as a still image first. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you click play. When you do, the video loads from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and data such as your IP address is sent to Google Ireland Limited. By clicking play you consent to this (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
